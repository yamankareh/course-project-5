## Ramadan Ramadan is a holy month in Islam. During Ramadan, Muslims fast from dawn until sunset and avoid eating and drinking during the fasting hours. It is a special time for prayer, reading the Quran, helping others, and showing kindness and patience. Fasting teaches Muslims self-discipline, gratitude, and compassion for people who are less fortunate. Families and friends often gather to share iftar, the meal eaten after sunset. Ramadan ends with Eid al-Fitr, a joyful celebration when Muslims come together to pray, visit loved ones, and share food and gifts. Ramadan is not only about fasting; it is also a time to strengthen faith, improve oneself, and spread peace and kindness.
